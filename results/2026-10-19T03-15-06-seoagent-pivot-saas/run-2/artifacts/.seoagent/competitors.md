# Competitors — lumina.example

## Competitor 1: Fireflies.ai — fireflies.ai
**Content focus:** Transcription, search across meetings, integrations catalog, admin/governance features.
**Coverage gaps:** Less depth on action-item follow-through and PM-tool sync workflows.
**Style:** Comparison-heavy ("X vs Y"), feature-matrix pages, free-tier-first messaging.

## Competitor 2: Otter.ai — otter.ai
**Content focus:** Live/real-time transcription, simplicity for small English-speaking teams.
**Coverage gaps:** Thinner on multi-platform (Teams/Zoom/Meet) parity and CRM/PM integration content.
**Style:** Feature-led landing pages, lighter on long-form educational content.

## Competitor 3: Grain — grain.com
**Content focus:** Go-to-market/sales teams, shareable clips, coaching playlists.
**Coverage gaps:** Not focused on general ops/product team workflows — an opening for non-sales use cases.
**Style:** Use-case-segmented comparison posts ("Fireflies vs Otter", etc.) targeting bottom-funnel comparison queries.

## Observed gap for Lumina
Most competitor content is transcription- and comparison-first. Fewer of them own the **"integration is the real ROI"** angle (action items synced to Slack/Notion/CRM) or write for ops/product/CS leads specifically rather than generic "meeting notes" searchers — both match Lumina's actual feature set (`/features` live page) and are open angles.
