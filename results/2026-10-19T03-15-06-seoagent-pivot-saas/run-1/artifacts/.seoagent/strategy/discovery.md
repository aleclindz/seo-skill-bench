# Strategy Discovery — Lumina

## Context

Lumina Labs pivoted from **InboxZap** (email cleanup / inbox zero) to **Lumina** (AI meeting assistant). The live site (`/`, `/features`, `/pricing`) is already repositioned around Lumina. The only ranking history in `gsc/` belongs entirely to InboxZap — see `.seoagent/strategy/migration-plan.md` for the per-asset disposition. This file covers the forward-looking keyword space for the new product (WebSearch-sourced, no DataForSEO — anonymous/local tier).

## Seed topics (from context.md + homepage)

AI meeting assistant, meeting transcription, meeting summaries, action items from meetings, Zoom/Meet/Teams integrations, Slack + Notion recap delivery.

## SERP observations (WebSearch, 2026-10-19)

- "ai meeting assistant" / "best ai meeting assistants" is dominated by incumbent vendors publishing their own roundups (Fireflies, Fathom, Read.ai, Granola, Tana, tl;dv, Otter, tl;dv) — competitive, but most "best of" posts are vendor-published, so a specific, well-sourced comparison is still winnable rather than a generic listicle.
- "ai meeting notes" / "ai meeting minutes" SERPs are a mix of vendor blogs (Fireflies, Fathom, Quill, Plaud) and general productivity sites (Atlassian) explaining the category — this is the more winnable entry point: informational intent, no single dominant authority site (no Wikipedia/government).
- A real split in the market: bot-based notetakers (join the call visibly) vs. bot-free/local-capture tools (Granola) — privacy/consent is a recurring theme across every roundup (announce-the-bot, consent law variance). Worth covering directly since Lumina is bot-based.

## Cluster priority

1. **ai-meeting-notes** (this session) — informational, lower competition than head "AI meeting assistant" terms, directly describes what Lumina's homepage already promises. PILLAR published.
2. **ai-meeting-assistant-comparisons** (next) — commercial-investigation intent ("vs" / "alternatives"), higher competition from incumbents with dedicated comparison content; sequence after the pillar has some traction.
