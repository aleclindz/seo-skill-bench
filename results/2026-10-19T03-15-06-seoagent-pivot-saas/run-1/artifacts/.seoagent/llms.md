# Lumina (Lumina Labs, Inc.)

> Lumina is an AI meeting assistant that joins calls, records and transcribes them, and delivers a summary with decisions and action items. Lumina Labs previously operated as **InboxZap**, an email-cleanup tool (bulk delete, unsubscribe, inbox zero) — the company has pivoted products and rebranded. Legacy InboxZap content/branding still lingers in parts of the codebase and is the top SEO risk (see Facts and Migration below).
