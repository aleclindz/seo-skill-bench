## Research notes / caveats

- Market-size claim seen in research ($1.42B in 2026 → $6.28B by 2035) is a third-party estimate (unsourced in the snippet) — do not cite as fact in content without verifying the original source.
- Several competitor "winner" comparison pages are vendor marketing (Fireflies' own "vs Fathom" page, Fathom's own "vs Otter" page) — do not treat their claims (ratings, feature gaps) as neutral fact; independent sources (G2, dev.to roundups) are the more defensible citations.
- G2 ratings observed: Fathom ~4.5/5 (56,400+ reviews), Fireflies ~4.7/5 in one comparison (749 reviews) / otherwise cited ~4.4-4.7 depending on source, Otter.ai ~4.4/5 (400-493 reviews depending on source) — figures varied slightly between search snippets; **re-verify exact current rating + review count directly on G2 at write-time** before quoting in any article.
- Legal/consent content (`is it legal to record meetings with ai`) touches jurisdiction-specific law (one-party vs two-party consent US states; Germany, Australia) — any article on this must carry a clear "not legal advice" disclaimer and should be fact-checked against current law at write-time, not just this research pass.
