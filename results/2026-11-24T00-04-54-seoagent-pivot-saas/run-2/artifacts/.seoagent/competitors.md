# Competitors — lumina.example

## Competitor 1: Otter.ai — otter.ai
**Domain authority signal:** Appears in nearly every "ai meeting assistant" and "ai meeting notes" SERP; frequently the #1 or #2 organic result for category terms. G2 rating ~4.4/5 (400+ reviews).
**Content focus:** Live/real-time transcription as the headline differentiator; heavy comparison-page production (Otter vs Fireflies, Otter vs Fathom, Otter vs Zoom AI Companion).
**Coverage gaps:** Comparison pages are vendor-marketing (self-serving "we win" framing); little honest-tradeoffs content; thin on Slack/Notion workflow automation angle.
**Style:** Short, feature-led landing pages; blog mixes SEO comparison posts with product-update announcements.

## Competitor 2: Fireflies.ai — fireflies.ai
**Domain authority signal:** Publishes its own large roundup ("AI Meeting Assistants Compared: 9 Tools") that ranks for broad category queries; G2 rating ~4.7/5 (749 reviews) in third-party comparisons.
**Content focus:** Cross-meeting context / "meeting intelligence" angle (search across all past meetings); Slack integration content ("send meeting notes & transcripts to Slack") is a direct overlap with Lumina's integration story.
**Coverage gaps:** Little content specifically on action-item *routing to owners* (Lumina's stated differentiator) vs. just storing/searching transcripts.
**Style:** Long-form listicles and comparison tables; aggressive internal linking from roundup posts to product pages.

## Competitor 3: Fathom — fathom.ai (fathom.video)
**Domain authority signal:** G2's top-rated tool in the category, 4.5/5 from 56,400+ reviews — strongest social-proof moat of the three.
**Content focus:** Simplicity / ease-of-setup positioning; free-tier-forward messaging (close to Lumina's "free for teams up to 5").
**Coverage gaps:** Less emphasis on multi-tool integration depth (Slack+Notion+CRM) than Fireflies; thinner blog footprint than Otter/Fireflies.
**Style:** Minimal, benefit-led pages; fewer long-form guides, more product-page comparisons.

## Competitor 4 (secondary watch): Fellow — fellow.app / fellow.ai
**Domain authority signal:** Publishes the deepest action-item/meeting-minutes educational content of the group (e.g. "How to Track Action Items: The Complete Guide").
**Content focus:** Governance/process angle — agendas, action-item follow-through, meeting minutes best practice — closer to Lumina's "decisions, blockers, action items" positioning than the transcription-first competitors.
**Coverage gaps:** Weaker on pure AI-transcription-quality content; positions itself more as meeting-management software than an AI note-taker.
**Style:** Process-driven long-form guides with templates; strong internal linking between a dedicated "Action Items" feature page and supporting blog content — a structure worth mirroring.
