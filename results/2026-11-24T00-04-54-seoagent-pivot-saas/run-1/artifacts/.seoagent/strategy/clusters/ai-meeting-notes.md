# Cluster: AI Meeting Notes

First forward-looking cluster for the post-pivot Lumina direction (ICP: ops/team leads running recurring video meetings). Chosen over a "best AI meeting assistant" comparison-style pillar because that term is dominated by established roundup sites (TechTarget, Zapier, ClickUp, G2 — see WebSearch research below); the informational cluster below has clearer, less crowded intent and links straight into product features (recording, transcription, action items).

**Research:** WebSearch on "ai meeting assistant" content landscape (2026) confirms recurring themes — action items with owner + deadline, searchable transcript libraries, meeting recaps — and that "best AI meeting assistant" roundups are vendor-affiliated/commercial. A second search on meeting-minutes best practice confirms a clear, stable template shape (header, agenda, decisions, action items, next meeting) with high, evergreen informational demand.
