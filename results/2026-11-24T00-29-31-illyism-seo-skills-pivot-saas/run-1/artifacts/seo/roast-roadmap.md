# SEO Roast Roadmap: lumina.example (2026-11-24)

Verdict: the product pivoted from InboxZap to Lumina and the site never finished moving — the homepage still told Google it sold inbox cleanup, the blog was 12 orphaned email posts rendered by a fetch to an API that doesn't exist, and the two pages that actually make money (`/features`, `/pricing`) didn't exist in the repo at all.

| # | Tier | Issue | Revenue impact | Fix | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FATAL | `/features` and `/pricing` had no route in the app — nav and sitemap pointed at pages that didn't exist in source | The two pages a buyer needs before paying were unbuildable/un-renderable | Added `app/features/page.tsx`, `app/pricing/page.tsx` with SSR content, metadata, canonical | agent | shipped in PR |
| 2 | FATAL | Root layout metadata (title/description/OG) still said "InboxZap — Clean Your Inbox in Minutes" while every page's actual content is about Lumina, an AI meeting assistant | Every search snippet and social share for the homepage described a different product than the one being sold; the single best-performing query in GSC ("inboxzap", 4.38% CTR) leads searchers to a page with zero acknowledgment of that brand | Rewrote `app/layout.tsx` metadata to Lumina; added `alternateName: "InboxZap"` to the Organization JSON-LD and a footer line so brand-search traffic isn't dead-ended | agent | shipped in PR |
| 3 | FATAL | `/blog` index was a client component fetching `/api/posts`, a route that doesn't exist (404) — the index rendered nothing but a permanent loading spinner, and the 12 existing posts were unlinked from any crawlable page and absent from the sitemap | Zero discoverability for all blog content; link equity on 12 already-indexed URLs wasn't being reinforced or passed anywhere | Rebuilt `/blog` as a server component reading static post data; added `app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` with `generateStaticParams`/`dynamicParams = false` so unknown slugs 404 cleanly instead of soft-404ing | agent | shipped in PR |
