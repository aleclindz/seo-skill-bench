# Competitors — Lumina (AI meeting assistant)

Identified via WebSearch across "best AI meeting assistant", "AI meeting notes app for teams", and comparison queries. This is a crowded, comparison-driven category — most top SERP results are vendor-written roundups (Fireflies, ClickUp, tl;dv, Zapier) rather than independent sites, which is itself the opportunity: alternative/comparison pages rank.

## Competitor 1: Fathom — fathom.video
**Positioning:** Free, unlimited recording/transcription/summaries. Frequently cited as the top free pick across 2026 roundups.
**Coverage gaps (for us to exploit):** No bot-free/local-recording option; free tier still the main draw, which caps differentiation on price alone.

## Competitor 2: Fireflies.ai — fireflies.ai
**Positioning:** Integration-heavy, 100+ language transcription, CRM/task-tool push (Slack, Notion, CRMs). Most-cited pick for teams with high meeting volume.
**Coverage gaps:** Own-blog comparison content dominates "best AI meeting assistant" SERPs — hard to out-rank head-on; alternative-style content ("fireflies alternative for small teams") is more winnable.

## Competitor 3: Otter.ai — otter.ai
**Positioning:** Real-time transcription, searchable meeting library, bot-free desktop recording option. ~$8.33/seat/month (annual).
**Coverage gaps:** Weaker on summarization/action-item automation vs. Fathom/Fireflies per reviews.

## Competitor 4: tl;dv — tldv.io
**Positioning:** Auto-sends AI-generated minutes to all participants post-call; publishes its own large comparison roundups (ranks well).

## Competitor 5: Grain — grain.com
**Positioning:** Sales/customer-success focus, automatic CRM updates. Narrower ICP than Lumina — not a direct competitor for general team meetings, but shows up in the same SERPs.

## Competitor 6: Fellow — fellow.app
**Positioning:** Repeatable meeting process/agenda tooling rather than a standalone notetaker — adjacent, not identical.

## Native / platform competitors
Microsoft Teams (Copilot notes) and Zoom Workplace AI Companion ship meeting notes natively — these cap demand for standalone tools among orgs already paying for Teams/Zoom's top tier. Worth acknowledging in comparison content ("do I need a separate AI notetaker if I have Zoom AI Companion?") rather than ignoring.

## What the SERP favors
Top results are dominated by listicle/roundup and comparison-table content from vendors themselves (per `keyword-research.md`'s SERP-pattern guide: "Top results = your competitors" → write pillar/sub-pillar/comparison content, not product pages). Head-on "best AI meeting assistant" terms are crowded; brand-vs-brand and segment-specific long-tails ("ai meeting assistant for teams up to 5", "fathom alternative with Slack integration") are the realistic wedge for a new entrant.
