# Lumina

> Lumina is an AI meeting assistant that joins calls, records and transcribes them, and turns them into summaries with decisions and action items — positioned as "never take meeting notes again." The site previously operated under a different brand/product, **InboxZap** (an email-cleanup / inbox-zero tool), and still carries that product's Google Search Console ranking history and several orphaned blog posts — see `.seoagent/strategy/migration-plan.md` for how that legacy equity is being handled.

## Pages

- [Lumina — The AI Meeting Assistant for Teams](https://lumina.example/): Lumina records, transcribes, and summarizes your team's meetings, then turns them into action items automatically. Free for teams up to 5.
- [Blog — Lumina](https://lumina.example/blog): The Lumina blog: articles on meetings, productivity, and teamwork.
